P — Point

Open with a clear claim. Name the author and the theme. Use a verb like conveys, highlights, explores.

E — Evidence

Quote directly from the text. Introduce with as [author] writes or in line X. Embed the quote naturally.

A — Analysis

Use this suggests / the word '…' connotes. Explain HOW the quote proves your point. Consider reader effect.

L — Link

End with Overall / Ultimately / Therefore and connect back to the question or thesis.
