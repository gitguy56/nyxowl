📖 PEAL Quick Guide
🎯

Point

Open with a clear, arguable claim. Use presents, explores, conveys, highlights to anchor your argument.

📌

Evidence

Quote directly from your source text. Embed short quotes naturally: the author writes "exact words".

🔍

Analysis

Use this suggests / this implies / the word '…' connotes to explain HOW the evidence supports your point.

🔗

Link

Close with Overall / Therefore / Ultimately and connect back to the question.
