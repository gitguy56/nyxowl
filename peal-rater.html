📖 PEAL Quick Guide
🎯

Point

Open with a clear, arguable claim. Use words like presents, explores, conveys, highlights to anchor your argument to the text.

📌

Evidence

Quote directly from the text ("exact words") or cite specific statistics, dates, or events. Embed short quotes naturally into your sentence.

🔍

Analysis

Use "this suggests / this implies / the word '…' connotes" to explain HOW and WHY the evidence supports your point. This is the most important part.

🔗

Link

Finish by connecting back to the question. Use "Overall / Therefore / Ultimately / This demonstrates that…" to close the loop.
